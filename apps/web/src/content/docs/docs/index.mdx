---
title: What is Pounce?
description: Pounce is a remote control for the coding agents running on your own machines — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor & opencode, all from your phone.
---

import { Card, CardGrid, LinkCard } from "@astrojs/starlight/components";

Pounce puts the coding agents running on your computers — **Claude Code, Codex,
Cursor, and opencode** — on your phone. Watch them work in real time, reply by
voice, answer their questions, review diffs, and ship, from anywhere.

It has three parts:

- **The phone app** — the remote. It shows every agent session across every
  machine you've paired, sorted by what needs your attention.
- **The Bridge** — a small program on your computer that hosts your agents and
  talks to the phone. It's built into the desktop app, bundled for
  Windows/Linux, and one `npx use-pounce` away in any terminal.
- **Nothing in the middle** — the app talks directly to your machine over
  Wi-Fi, or through Pounce's own secure peer-to-peer tunnel when you're away.
  There's no account and no Pounce server relaying your code.
