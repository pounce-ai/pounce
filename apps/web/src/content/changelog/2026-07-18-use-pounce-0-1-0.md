The new `use-pounce` CLI is on npm. One command starts the Bridge in the
background, prints a pairing QR right in your terminal, and waits for your
scan.

- Works **over SSH** — the QR carries the machine's tunnel identity, so you
  can pair a remote server your phone shares no network with. No VPN, no
  port-forwarding.
- Random per-machine pairing token, kept in `~/.pounce`.
- `pounce status`, `pounce logs`, `pounce stop`, `pounce qr` subcommands, plus
  `--lan` for Wi-Fi-only pairing.
