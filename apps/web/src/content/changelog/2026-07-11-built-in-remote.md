date: 2026-07-11

The Bridge now brings up Pounce's own secure peer-to-peer tunnel
automatically, and the pairing QR carries everything the phone needs to reach
that machine from any network. Pair once on Wi-Fi, then steer your agents from
the gym, the train, anywhere — no VPN, no port-forwarding, no third-party
relay account.
