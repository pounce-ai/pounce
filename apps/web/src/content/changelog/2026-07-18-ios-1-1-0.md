- **Search everywhere.** Search across every machine, project, and
  conversation in your fleet — or within a single thread — and jump straight
  to the matching message.
- **Cursor support.** Sessions from the Cursor CLI (`cursor-agent`) now show
  up alongside Claude Code, Codex, and opencode — with full history.
- Assorted streaming and scrolling fixes for long, busy sessions.
